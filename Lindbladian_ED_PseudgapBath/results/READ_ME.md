# Phase 2 (Step 1b) Verification Guide: Two Baths Connected & Cutoff Stress-Testing

This section explains how to read the console output (`phase2_summary_table.txt`) and the 8-panel figure (`phase2_verification.png`) generated by `run_phase2_benchmarks.py`.

## 1. The Two-Bath Tight-Binding + Lindblad Setup

In Step 1b, the resonant quantum dot at energy $\epsilon_d$ is coupled simultaneously to **two** discretized tight-binding baths (`LyapunovSolver([bath_PG, bath_S])` with $M = 1 + 10,000 + 4,000 = 14,001$ single-particle modes):

$$H = \epsilon_d d^\dagger d + \sum_{q \in \text{PG}} \epsilon_{\text{PG},q} c_{\text{PG},q}^\dagger c_{\text{PG},q} + \sum_{k \in S} \epsilon_{S,k} c_{S,k}^\dagger c_{S,k} + \sum_{\alpha \in \{\text{PG}, S\}} \sum_{q \in \alpha} v_{\alpha q} \left(d^\dagger c_{\alpha q} + c_{\alpha q}^\dagger d\right)$$

1. **Power-Law Bath (`"PG"`, $N_{\text{PG}} = 10,000$ modes):** Hybridization $\Gamma_{\text{PG}}(\omega) = \Gamma_0 |\omega|^r \Theta(D_{\text{PG}} - |\omega|)$, held at a fixed low temperature $T_{\text{PG}}$ (with optional infrared regularization $\omega_{\text{IR}}$).
2. **Probe Bath (`"S"`, $N_S = 4,000$ modes):** Hybridization $\Gamma_S(\omega) = \Gamma_{S,0} \, g_S(\omega) \, \Theta(D_S - |\omega|)$, held at temperature $T_S$.
3. **Non-Equilibrium Protocol Operation:** We vary **only** the probe bath temperature $T_S$ (holding $T_{\text{PG}}$ fixed), solve the differentiated continuous Lyapunov equation for $\frac{\partial n_d(\epsilon_d, T_S)}{\partial T_S}$, and integrate over the dot energy $\epsilon_d' \in [-\epsilon_{\max}, \epsilon_d]$:
   $$\tilde{S}(\epsilon_d) \equiv -\int_{-\epsilon_{\max}}^{\epsilon_d} \frac{\partial n_d(\epsilon_d', T_S)}{\partial T_S}\, d\epsilon_d'$$

## 2. How the Analytical Benchmarks (`Eq. 11`, `Eq. 12`, `Eq. 14`) Relate to `Lyap`

* **`Exact Two-Bath Continuum (Note Eq. 11)`:**
  Integrates the exact Keldysh derivative $\frac{\partial n_d}{\partial T_S} = \int d\omega\, A_{\text{tot}}(\epsilon_d; \omega) \frac{\Gamma_S(\omega)}{\Gamma_{\text{PG}}(\omega) + \Gamma_S(\omega)} \frac{\partial f_S(\omega; T_S)}{\partial T_S}$ over $\epsilon_d' \in [-\epsilon_{\max}, \epsilon_d]$, keeping $\Gamma_S(\omega)$ in the denominator and retaining finite bandwidths $(D_{\text{PG}}, D_S)$.
* **`Note Eq. (12)` ($\Gamma_S \ll \Gamma_{\text{PG}}(\omega)$, $D \to \infty$):**
  Replaces the branching ratio $\frac{\Gamma_S}{\Gamma_{\text{PG}}(\omega) + \Gamma_S}$ by $\frac{\Gamma_S}{\Gamma_0 |\omega|^r}$ while keeping the full temperature dependence inside $\arctan\big(\tan(\frac{\pi r}{2}) + \frac{(x T_S)^{1-r}}{\Gamma_0}\big)$.
* **`Note Eq. (14)` (Low-$T_S$ Asymptotic Power Law):**
  At low probe temperatures ($(x T_S)^{1-r} \ll \Gamma_0$), the non-equilibrium integral scales as:
  $$\frac{\tilde{S}(0)}{\ln 2} = \frac{\Gamma_{S,0}}{\Gamma_0} \frac{\alpha(r)}{T_S^r} \, r, \qquad \alpha(r) \equiv \frac{1}{\ln 2}\int_0^\infty dx \frac{x^{1-r}}{(1+e^x)(1+e^{-x})}$$
* **Reconstructed Equilibrium Entropy $S_{\text{recon}}(0) / \ln 2$:**
  Dividing $\tilde{S}(0) / \ln 2$ by the analytical prefactor $\left(\frac{\Gamma_{S,0}}{\Gamma_0}\right) \alpha(r) T_S^{-r}$ defines:
  $$\frac{S_{\text{recon}}(0)}{\ln 2} \equiv \frac{\tilde{S}(0) / \ln 2}{\left(\frac{\Gamma_{S,0}}{\Gamma_0}\right) \alpha(r) T_S^{-r}} \underset{\text{Eq. (14)}}{\longrightarrow} r$$
  Whenever Eq. (14) holds, $S_{\text{recon}}(0)/\ln 2$ collapses directly onto the horizontal target values $r \in \{-0.9, -0.5, -0.3, 0.0, +0.3, +0.5, +0.9\}$.

## 3. Panel-by-Panel Guide to `phase2_verification.png`

### Row 1: Core Two-Bath Protocol & Scaling (Panels a–d)

* **Panel (a): Rescaled Probe Temperature Derivative $\frac{\Gamma_0}{\Gamma_S} T_S^r \frac{\partial n_d}{\partial T_S}$ vs. $\epsilon_d / D$ ($T_S / D = 0.002$)**
  * **Operation:** Shows the steady-state occupation sensitivity $\partial n_d / \partial T_S$ when only the probe bath temperature $T_S$ is varied, multiplied by $(\Gamma_0/\Gamma_S) T_S^r$ so all 7 exponents can be compared on the same vertical scale.
* **Panel (b): Rescaled Two-Bath Integral $\tilde{S}(\epsilon_d) / [\frac{\Gamma_S}{\Gamma_0}\alpha(r)T_S^{-r}\ln 2]$ vs. $\epsilon_d / D$ ($T_S / D = 0.002$)**
  * **Operation:** Running gate-voltage integral of Panel (a) from $-\epsilon_{\max}$ to $\epsilon_d$, showing that the discrete tight-binding Lyapunov integral (solid colored lines) matches the exact two-bath continuum integral (dashed black lines) and converges to $r$ at $\epsilon_d = 0$ (dotted horizontal lines).
* **Panel (c): Power-Law Scaling of $|\tilde{S}(0)| / (\frac{\Gamma_S}{\Gamma_0}\ln 2)$ vs. Probe Temperature $T_S / D$ (Log-Log)**
  * **Operation:** Log-log plot across 3 orders of magnitude in $T_S / D$. Positive exponents ($r > 0$, pseudogap) diverge as $T_S^{-r}$ as $T_S \to 0$, whereas negative exponents ($r < 0$, divergent DOS / AOC) decrease as $T_S^{+|r|}$.
* **Panel (d): Reconstructed Entropy $S_{\text{recon}}(0) / \ln 2$ vs. Exponent $r$ at $T_S / D = 0.002$ (with $\alpha(r)$ Inset)**
  * **Operation:** Compares the reconstructed entropy $S_{\text{recon}}(0)/\ln 2$ from the two-bath tight-binding Lyapunov solver (`o`) and Note Eq. (12) (`s`) against the exact linear law $S(0)/\ln 2 = r$ (`--`). The inset displays the universal prefactor $\alpha(r)$ from Note Eq. (14).

### Row 2: Temperature Window & Explicit Cutoff Stress Tests (Panels e–h)

* **Panel (e): Reconstructed Entropy $S_{\text{recon}}(0) / \ln 2$ vs. Probe Temperature $T_S / D$**
  * **Operation:** Divides the data of Panel (c) by $\alpha(r) T_S^{-r}$, showing flat low-temperature plateaus at $S_{\text{recon}}(0)/\ln 2 = r$ across 2.5 to 3 decades of $T_S / D$.
* **Panel (f) [Cutoff Test 1]: Breakdown of Weak Probe Coupling ($\Gamma_S / \Gamma_0$ Sweep)**
  * **Operation:** Sweeps the probe coupling ratio $\Gamma_S / \Gamma_0$ over 4 decades ($10^{-5} \to 10^{-1}$) at $T_S / D = 0.002$.
  * **Key physical insight:** For $r < 0$ (divergent DOS), $\Gamma_{\text{PG}}(T_S) = \Gamma_0 T_S^{-|r|}$ is large at low temperatures, so $\Gamma_S \ll \Gamma_{\text{PG}}(T_S)$ holds even up to $\Gamma_S / \Gamma_0 \sim 10^{-1}$. For $r > 0$ (pseudogap), $\Gamma_{\text{PG}}(T_S) = \Gamma_0 T_S^r$ is tiny at low temperatures, so $S_{\text{recon}}(0)$ bends toward zero as soon as $\Gamma_S$ approaches $\Gamma_{\text{PG}}(T_S) = \Gamma_0 T_S^r$.
* **Panel (g) [Cutoff Test 2]: Finite Power-Law Bath Temperature $T_{\text{PG}} > 0$ & AOC Thermal IR Cutoff $\omega_{\text{IR}} = \pi T_{\text{PG}}$**
  * **Operation:** Holds the power-law bath at finite $T_{\text{PG}} / D = 0.002$ while scanning $T_S / D$.
  * **Key physical insight:** In a strictly non-interacting bath with fixed spectrum $\Gamma_0|\omega|^r$ (dashed curves), $f_{\text{PG}}(\omega; T_{\text{PG}})$ drops out of $\partial n_d / \partial T_S$. However, when finite $T_{\text{PG}}$ regularizes the power-law bath spectrum below $\omega_{\text{IR}} = \pi T_{\text{PG}}$ (as in an interacting AOC/Kondo bath, circles + solid curves), the $T_S^{-r}$ scaling cuts off as soon as $T_S$ drops below $\pi T_{\text{PG}}$ (vertical dotted line).
* **Panel (h) [Cutoff Test 3]: Frequency-Dependent Probe $\Gamma_S(\omega)$ & Probe Bandwidth Cutoff $D_S$**
  * **Operation:** Compares a wide-band flat probe ($D_S = D$, solid lines) against a Lorentzian probe $\Gamma_S(\omega) = \frac{\Gamma_{S,0}}{1 + (\omega/\Omega_S)^2}$ ($\Omega_S = 0.015 D$, open squares) and a narrow-bandwidth probe ($D_S = 0.025 D$, open triangles).
  * **Key physical insight:** Confirms that Eq. (14) depends only on the zero-frequency value $\Gamma_S(0)$ as long as $T_S \ll \min(\Omega_S, D_S)$, and rolls off cleanly when the thermal window $|\omega| \sim 4T_S$ reaches $\Omega_S$ or $D_S$.
